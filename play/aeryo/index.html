Help & Legal

How to play

  • Tap an arrow. If nothing is in its way, it slides off the board.
  • From Level 5, tapping a blocked arrow costs 1 Focus. At zero Focus, the level simply restarts.
  • Trace shows one arrow’s path and what stops it. Hint points to an arrow that can leave now.
  • Clear a board without losing Focus and without Hint or Trace for Perfect Flow.
  • Daily Flow brings one board each day. Complete every day of a month to earn its Flow Seal.
